Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Decibel Meter: Noise Detector ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect and Permission Usage

Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Our application requires access to your device's microphone solely to measure real-time ambient noise levels (decibels). Audio data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or uploaded to any external server.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or audio recordings. All decibel calculations happen entirely on your local device.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version of the application does not utilize third-party analytics or advertising services that collect personal data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
