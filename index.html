Privacy Policy

App: Decibel Meter: Noise Detector | Effective Date: October 2026

🔒 Zero Audio Recording & Zero Data Collection Guarantee: Sound Pollution Detector processes sound levels locally in real-time on your device. We do NOT record, save, store, or transmit your voice or background audio to any server.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Decibel Meter: Noise Detector ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles device permissions, processes audio data, and manages user privacy.

2. Permissions Requested & Purpose

To provide accurate noise monitoring, our app requests the following Android system permissions:

  • Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively to sample ambient sound pressure levels and calculate real-time noise volume in decibels (dB SPL / dBA). Audio samples are processed in volatile device memory (RAM) and immediately discarded. No audio recordings or sound files are ever created or stored.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Allows continuous noise level monitoring while the app is running in the background or when your device screen is off. An active persistent notification is displayed whenever foreground monitoring is active so you retain full control over when the microphone is in use.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13 and above to display the active noise status notification bar and provide quick control actions (such as stopping continuous sound monitoring).
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly to perform automated version compatibility checks and retrieve mandatory app updates via secure HTTP connections.

3. Local Storage & Calibration Data

Our app saves a small microphone calibration offset value (SharedPreferences) locally on your device. This allows the app to retain your customized decibel calibration settings across sessions. This data remains 100% private to your device and is never uploaded or shared.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not collect, sell, trade, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or device data with third parties. The app interacts with the following service solely for essential app functionality:

  • App Version Updates: Fetches minimal JSON configuration metadata via Retrofit to inform users when a mandatory app update is available. No user or device identification data is included in these update requests.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal data from children under the age of 13. The app functions as a general utility tool suitable for users of all ages.
